Pass & play

Two players, one screen. No code, no waiting — just take turns.

Each player starts on opposite sides of a 9×9 board. On your turn, either step your pawn one square toward the far edge, or spend one of your ten walls to block your opponent's route. Walls span two squares and can never seal off someone's path completely — there's always a way through. If your pawn lands next to your opponent, you can hop over them. First pawn to touch the opposite edge wins.
